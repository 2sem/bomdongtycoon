## Competitive Landscape Notes

- **로드마마** (4.1 stars, 12 reviews): Closest direct competitor — Korean street food stall with RPG elements. Weak ASO and thin review base = clear opportunity.
- **생과일 타이쿤** (3.8 stars): Nostalgia-driven, but slow and story-heavy. Players want faster idle loop.
- **고양이 오피스** (4.7 stars, 13k reviews): Benchmark for idle/방치형 in KR market. Key signals: "방치형", "힐링", "시뮬레이션" in subtitle converts strongly.
- **Idle Restaurant Tycoon** (4.7 stars, 17k reviews): Global benchmark. Offline earning hook ("earn online AND offline") is the #1 conversion driver in descriptions.
- **Market gap**: No idle tycoon game owns the "authentic Korean seasonal vegetable + 포장마차" niche. 봄동's seasonal theme (spring→summer→winter) is a unique differentiator.
