🥬 Bomdong Tycoon — Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

Bomdong Tycoon ("the App") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App collects and how it is handled.

1. Information Collected

The developer (Leesam) does not directly collect personal information. However, the following third-party services may collect data:

  • Google AdMob: May collect advertising identifiers (IDFA), device information, and app usage data to serve ads.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Collects device tokens to send push notifications. No account information is collected.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To show personalized or non-personalized ads (AdMob)
  • To deliver in-game notifications (FCM)

3. Information Sharing

The developer does not sell or share user data with third parties. Google AdMob and Firebase process data under their own privacy policies.

  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Data Storage

The App stores game progress data (coins, staff status, etc.) locally on your device (UserDefaults only). No personal data is sent to external servers by the developer.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your Choices

To limit ad tracking, go to device Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
